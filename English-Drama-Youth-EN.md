## ENGLISH DRAMA YOUTH (10–17)

**YEAR-END SHOWCASE · FINAL PERFORMANCE**

*Creative Drama & Stage — For Young People*

- **DURATION:** 8 months · 1 day/week (September–May)
- **VENUE:** Pera & Kadıköy
- **CAPACITY:** 12 people
- **START:** Sunday, September 27

A program for young people that combines creative drama and stagework with English learning. Designed for participants aged 10–17, it closes with a final showcase in front of an audience at the end of the year. October–May, one day a week.

**OCTOBER–DECEMBER · Discovery & Play**
Improvisation, body–voice–imagination exercises. Confidence in English. Group dynamics and stage confidence.

**JANUARY–MARCH · Character & Text**
Scene text work, character-building, partner work. Verbal and physical expression. English dramatic text.

**APRIL–MAY · Final Showcase**
Rehearsal process and a final performance in front of an audience. A real stage experience, with a real audience.
