## BROADWAY MUSICAL DANCE

**BROADWAY MUSICAL DANCE**

*Jazz · Theatre Dance · Choreography*

- **DURATION:** 12 weeks or 6 weeks
- **VENUE:** Kadıköy & Taksim
- **CAPACITY:** 15 people
- **START:** Kadıköy Thursday, October 1 · Taksim Pera Saturday, October 3 · 19:00
- **LEAD INSTRUCTOR:** Köksal Ünal

An intensive program teaching the dance language of Broadway musical theatre. Stage choreography and combination work through jazz and theatre dance technique. Two options: the full 12-week program or a 6-week short program. No dance experience required — we start from technical fundamentals.

**WEEKS 1–4 · Technical Fundamentals**
Jazz and theatre dance basics: alignment, rhythm, coordination.

**WEEKS 5–8 · Choreography & Style**
Scenes from the Broadway repertoire. Style and group choreography.

**WEEKS 9–12 · Advanced Choreography**
Long combinations and synchronisation — one choreography, start to finish.
