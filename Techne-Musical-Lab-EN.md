## TECHNE MUSICAL LAB

**DRAMA · THEATRE · MUSICAL**

*Stage. Voice. Movement. — In Front of an Audience.*

- **DURATION:** 8 months · 2 days/week (September–May)
- **VENUE:** Kadıköy
- **CAPACITY:** 12 people
- **START:** Monday, September 28
- **LEAD INSTRUCTORS:** Köksal Ünal & Sitare Bilge

An 8-month comprehensive program that builds musical staging on top of a foundation in drama and theatre. The program begins with acting exercises and dramaturgical work, then completes stage integration with vocal and dance disciplines. It closes with a graduating performance in front of an audience. Applicants are expected to submit a short video of themselves performing a musical or pop song; acceptance is based on video review.

**OCTOBER–DECEMBER · Drama & Acting**
Stage presence, character-building, and dramaturgical work. Theatre's fundamental tools: body, voice, and text. The actor's voice meeting vocal technique.

**JANUARY–MARCH · Musical Staging**
Musical rhythm, Broadway dance fundamentals, and stage space. Choreography and musical text work built on a drama foundation.

**APRIL–MAY · Graduating Performance**
Not a song collage — a staged musical. Full production with costume, lighting, set, and a live audience. Personal piece development and group staging run in parallel.
