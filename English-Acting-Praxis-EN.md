## ENGLISH ACTING PRAXIS

**ECE ERTEZ · HARIKA UYGUR MASTERCLASS**

*For Actors Who Want to Take Their Craft to an International Stage*

- **DURATION:** 12 weeks
- **VENUE:** Pera
- **CAPACITY:** 14 people
- **START:** Saturday, September 26 · 11:00
- **LEAD INSTRUCTOR:** Ece Ertez

A twelve-week stop for anyone who wants to test their acting on international ground. You can be a working actor, a student still in training, or simply curious about the stage — B1-level English, enough to express yourself in everyday life, is all you need.

Two things train in parallel over the twelve weeks: the actor's instrument, and the actor's command of the language. Text selection, character-building, rehearsal discipline — all of it runs in English. The language stops being a memorized line and becomes the actor's tool.

The program closes with a one-day masterclass and filming day led by casting director Harika Uygur; performances are recorded and delivered to participants.

**WEEKS 1–4 · Text & Character**
Text selection, analysis, character motivation. Subtext and scene intention.

**WEEKS 5–8 · Rehearsal Process**
Partner work, blocking, scene dynamics. Real rehearsal discipline.

**WEEKS 9–12 · Integration & Harika Uygur Masterclass**
Integration work and personal feedback sessions. The program closes with a one-day masterclass by casting director Harika Uygur: live performances are watched, recorded, and delivered to participants.
