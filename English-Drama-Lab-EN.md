## ENGLISH DRAMA LAB

**ENGLISH CREATIVE DRAMA**

*Creative Drama · Improvisation · English*

- **DURATION:** 12 weeks
- **VENUE:** Pera & Kadıköy
- **CAPACITY:** 12 people
- **START:** Saturday, September 12 · 15:00
- **LEAD INSTRUCTORS:** Alara Lokum, Ece Ertez & Yeşim Çelebi

English settles into the body and voice through creative drama exercises and improvisation. Free of line memorization, this program relies on instant reaction and imagination, inviting participants into direct contact with English expression. We combine the practice of a conversation club with the tools of creative drama: conversation opens up on stage, through the body and through play.

**WEEKS 1–4 · Warm-Up & Discovery**
Play and trust exercises, language games, the body–voice–imagination triangle. Opening up English intuition.

**WEEKS 5–8 · Improvisation & Character**
On-the-spot scene work, status games, character improvisations. Speaking the language without thinking about it.

**WEEKS 9–12 · Integration & Application**
Bringing the tools acquired together on stage. Deepening group improvisation and in-the-moment scene work.
