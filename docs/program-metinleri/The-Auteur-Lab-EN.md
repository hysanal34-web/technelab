## THE AUTEUR LAB

**CREATIVE WRITING · DRAMATURGY**

*From Impulse to Design — From Design to Action*

- **DURATION:** 8 weeks (per module)
- **VENUE:** Kadıköy
- **CAPACITY:** 10 people
- **START:** Wednesday, October 7
- **LEAD INSTRUCTOR:** Halil Yağız Şanal

A reading line running from Sophocles to Beckett. We look at how texts are built, and what narrative has kept — and shed — across centuries. This is a beginner's program, but it doesn't start with technique: first you need to see why a text works. Once that eye settles in, which form you write in becomes secondary — the novel, the screenplay, and the play all feed from the same ground.

The program has three modules, eight weeks each. Modules can be taken separately: you can start with the first and decide on continuing afterward.

**WEEKS 1–8 · Module 1: From Antiquity to Modern**
From Sophocles to Ibsen: dramatic structure, character arc, conflict and subtext.

**WEEKS 9–16 · Module 2: Contemporary Writing**
Beckett, Kane, Zeller, Williams. Fragmented structures and plural narrative.

**WEEKS 17–24 · Module 3: Your Own Voice**
Finding your own voice. Short play drafts and dramaturgical feedback.
