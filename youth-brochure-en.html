English Drama Youth

Language as experience. Expression as craft.
Programme Overview

English Drama Youth is designed for young people aged 10–17 who want to explore the English language through theatre. Rather than grammar drills, we work through scenes, monologues, movement, and ensemble play — discovering how English sounds, feels, and lives in performance.

By the end of the programme, participants will have performed, collaborated on scenes, and developed confidence speaking English in a creative, low-pressure environment.

What We Do

Scene work: Short scripts, monologues, and improvisation in English.

Movement & voice: How bodies and accents shape meaning.

Ensemble: Collaborative storytelling and group performance.

Reflection: Thinking about language, culture, and character through drama.

Course Structure

Weeks 1–2: Ice-breakers, character exploration, vocal warm-ups.

Weeks 3–5: Scene analysis, ensemble play, improvisations on themes.

Weeks 6–7: Preparing for final showcase.

Week 8: Final performance for friends and family.
